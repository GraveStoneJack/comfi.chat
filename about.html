About ComfiChat

Simple online chat for meeting new people.

What ComfiChat Is

ComfiChat is a chat site built for quick conversations, anonymous chat, and live one-to-one chats with people online. It is designed for people who want a simple way to talk to strangers, join chat rooms, and meet new people without a complicated setup.

How It Works

You can create a lightweight profile or start with the talk-to-strangers flow. ComfiChat uses basic details like username, age, gender, and country to help people decide who they want to chat with. Once you are online, you can see other available users and start a private conversation.

What You Can Use It For

  • Anonymous chat with people online.
  • Random conversations when you want someone new to talk to.
  • Chat rooms and live online chats for casual conversation.
  • Meeting strangers while keeping control with blocking and reporting tools.

Safety And Moderation

ComfiChat includes tools for reporting, blocking, and moderation review. The goal is to keep conversations respectful, reduce spam and abuse, and give users practical controls when a chat does not feel right.
